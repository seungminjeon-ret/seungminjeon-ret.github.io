Seungmin Jeon

Ph.D. student at KAIST

I study programming languages and compilers to make quantum algorithms easier to express and implement. My recent work focuses on quadratic speedups; I am also interested in high-level approaches to superquadratic speedups and QEC-aware compilation.

I am part of the Programming Language Research Group, advised by Sukyoung Ryu and co-advised by Jeehoon Kang.

Publications

Quantum Monte Carlo Estimation via Probabilistic Programming

OOPSLA 2026

Quantum Probabilistic Model Checking for Time-Bounded Properties

Seungmin Jeon, Kyeongmin Cho, Chan Gu Kang, Janggun Lee, Hakjoo Oh, Jeehoon Kang

OOPSLA 2024

[Paper][Artifact][DOI]

TL;DR: We developed QPMC to apply quantum amplitude estimation to probabilistic model checking, giving a quadratic improvement in accuracy dependence over statistical sampling. Compact system models are compiled directly into circuits, avoiding explicit state-space enumeration.

Memento: A Framework for Detectable Recoverability in Persistent Memory

Kyeongmin Cho, Seungmin Jeon, Azalea Raad, Jeehoon Kang

PLDI 2023

[Paper][Artifact][Code]

TL;DR: We introduced Memento to recover concurrent data structures after crashes without duplicating operation effects. Its compositional framework removes the need for custom recovery code for each structure, with performance comparable to hand-tuned persistent implementations.
